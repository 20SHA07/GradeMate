Course Learning Outcomes
• Draw free-body diagrams and solve for forces and moments in both static and dynamic systems
• Understand and describe the various mechanical properties of materials
• Describe and analyze the motion of particles and rigid bodies
• Identify, formulate and solve problems involving kinematic/kinetic variables using equations of
motion, principles of impulse and momentum, and principles of work and energy
• Obtain stress-strain distributions in organic and synthetic materials under various loading modes
• Work in teams and communicate effectively
