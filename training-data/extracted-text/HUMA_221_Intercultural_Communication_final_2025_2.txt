HUMA 221 Intercultural Communication 
(3 Lecture, 3 Credits)

Prerequisite: ENGL 102 

Course Catalog Description:
This course identifies and delineates the communication skills needed for effective interaction in a global society; examines the relationship between communication, language and culture; and studies the general concepts of intercultural communication.

Textbook: 
An introduction to intercultural communication: Identities in a global community. F. Jandt, (2025). 8th edition. SAGE Publications, ISBN: 9781071887110

Reference Materials: 
Communicating between cultures. (2016). 9th edition. Cengage ISBN-10. 1285444620. 

Course Topics: 
Introduction to defining culture and communication. (3 lectures)
Perception and intercultural communication. (2 lectures)
Barriers to intercultural communication. (2 lectures)
Intercultural communication and verbal communication. (2 lectures)
Intercultural communication and non-verbal communication. (2 lectures)
Dimensions of culture. (4 lectures)
Comparative cultural patterns. (4 lectures)
Rhetorical patterns and culture. (3 lectures)
History and cultural patterns. (2 lectures) 
Education and diversity. (2 lectures)
Managing intercultural conflict. (2 lectures)
Intercultural communication practices: managing, negotiating and persuading. (2 lectures)

Structure and Learning Methodologies:
Lectures are 2 x 75 minutes per week. A combination of white board use, PowerPoint slide presentation, LMS and interactive class discussions utilizes to encourage student participation and enhance learning.
