Course Catalog Description: This course introduces ordinary differential equations with linear algebra. The
course covers basic topics of linear algebra, including linear systems, basic properties of matrices, vector
spaces, and eigenvalues and eigenvectors. The course also covers solution techniques for first‐order
differential equations, higher‐order linear equations with constant coefficients, linear and almost linear
systems of differential equations, and Laplace transforms.

Course Learning Outcomes
1 Solve systems of linear equations.
2 Compute eigenvalues, Eigenvectors and Eigenspaces.
3 Apply the concepts of vector spaces to the solution of ordinary differential equations.
4 Solve real world problems using ordinary differential equations and systems of differential equations.
5 Solve initial value problems using Laplace transforms.

Additional Information
Assessments
There are four quizzes scheduled for this course, counting for 20% of the final grade. Online Homework will
count for 10%, a Project for 10%, a Midterm for 25% and a Final Exam for 35%.
Calculator Policy
You may use a basic scientific calculator to help you with arithmetic computations in this course. The calculator
must not have a graphing capability. The calculator must not be capable of performing symbolic manipulation.
The calculator must not be capable of computing derivatives and integrals. A good example of an appropriate
calculator is the Casio fx‐82ES.
You will be permitted to use your (basic) calculator in the Quizzes, Midterm Examination and the Final
Examination.
Mobile phones may not be used as calculators in the Quizzes, Midterm Examination or Final Examination.
Mobile phones must be switched off during quizzes and exams and must be put away for the duration of the
quiz/exam.
Classroom Policies
Discipline is one of the main components of your academic and professional success. Please make every effort to
arrive at the class periods in a timely fashion. Students arriving late to class are a distraction to both the instructor
and the other students in the classroom. If students require the class periods to end on time then the class periods
must begin on time.
If you are absent from any class period it is your responsibility to make‐up the missed material.
Grading
Writing correct solutions is essential in the life of a scientist, including the correct use of the mathematical
symbols. Not only one needs to be understood by others, but only by paying attention to what is written a
student can acquire a good understanding of the concepts. Therefore, in all assessments, the correctness of the
solution writing will be evaluated and marks will be deducted for mistakes (notations, clarity, structure of the
solution, etc)
For quizzes and midterm, students have TWO DAYS after they get back their copy to check if there are any
grading mistakes, and if so, ask for revision. After two days, the result will be considered final and no more
revision will be allowed.
Conflict Resolution Procedure
For any issue, the following procedure needs to be followed:
1) students need to talk first to their instructor;
2) if (1) did not resolve the issue, students can talk to the course coordinator;
3) if (1) and (2) did not resolve the issue, students can talk to the chair of the math department;
4) if none of the above resolved the issue, students can talk to the Associate dean of undergraduate studies.
5) No Grade Appeal is approved if the student has not seen the copy of his/her final exam.
There will be no leniency with phones and any other electronics during exams.
