HUMA 229 - Critical Thinking
(3 Lecture, – 3 Credits) 
 
Prerequisite: ENGL 102
Co-requisite: None
 
Course Catalog Description:
This course introduces students to the principles and practices of critical thinking. Through analysis, evaluation, and reasoning, students will help develop the cognitive skills and dispositions necessary to engage in clear, logical, and independent thought. Emphasis will be placed on applying these skills in academic, professional, and everyday contexts.
 
Textbooks:

Course Topics: 
1-Critique vs Criticism (2 lectures)
2-Traits of a Critical Thinker (2 lectures)
3- Inferences VS Assumption: Science vs Pseudoscience (2 lectures)
4- How Do We Know What We Know I: Reading and Discussion/Group Work (4 lectures)
5- Deductive and Inductive Reasoning: Reading and Class Work (2 lectures)
6- Evaluating Arguments I: Reading and Discussion/Group Work (4 lectures)
7-Major Fallacies: Reading Discussion/Group Work/ Class Activities (4 lectures)
8-The Scientific Method: Opportunities/Challenges/Limitations (4 lectures)
9- Triangulation and Alternative Perspectives (2 lectures)
10- Debating Contemporary Challenges I: Anthropocentrism, Ecocentrism and AI (4 lectures)

Structure and Learning Methodologies:
Lectures will be 2 x 75-minute sessions per week. Instruction will be a combination of lectures/seminar discussions, in-class composing/writing, and small-group discussions/debating. Skills introduced in this Critical Thinking course will be extended and developed.

Additional Grading Policy Information:
Plagiarism: 
 Students who are found to plagiarize (copy in any form including using generative AI) text, images, or other materials at any percentage of the total volume of an assignment will receive a warning and one opportunity to completely revise their work. The maximum students can receive for a revised assessment is 80%.

A second instance of plagiarism will result in an F on the assignment and a report to the Academic Integrity Committee. Students are advised to consult the Handbook for all policies related to plagiarism and other forms of cheating.

Grade Review Timeframe: 
After assessment marks are announced, students have one week to request further clarification of the grade awarded. After the one-week period, no further grade discussions will be considered.
