Instructor Policy on late submission of assignments:
 Attendance: You are responsible for what you missed.
 Homework: Homework is due 7 days after date assigned. If not completed during this time you
will get a “0”. No extension unless there is an excused absence from the registrar’s office.
 Model: Modeling section is a large component of the course grade. You are responsible for
designing and completing your model. The Model White Paper will be assessed for hypothesis
and problem statement, subject development, correct grammar, and model description.
 Quizzes: Two quizzes will be held and will take up 20% of the grade
 Exams: There are no make-up exams unless there is an excused absence from the registrar’s
office. You will get a “0” if you miss an exam with an unexcused absence.
 Plagiarism and Cheating: Zero tolerance of any types of academic dishonesty.
