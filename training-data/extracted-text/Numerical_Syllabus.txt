Instructor Policy on late submission of assignments:
• Attendance: Mandatory, 3.5% of the final grade accounts for attendance and class participation.
• Active engagement in class evaluation constitutes 0.5% of the overall final grade
• 1% of the final grade is allocated for opportunities such as extra credit and at the instructor's
discretion.
• A student may be absent for no more than 7% of contact time (2 classes) from a course with no
penalty, provided that the student’s absence does not result in him/her missing any items of
assessment (quizzes, exams, turning in homework, etc.).
• For unexcused absences beyond 7% of contact time (after 2 classes), a penalty of 0.5% will be
deducted from your total mark (out of 100%) for each unexcused absence up to 7 classes.
• The weekly homework submission should be handled electronically via the Blackboard, so you'll
need to scan your homework as pdf file before uploading it
