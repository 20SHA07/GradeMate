COSC 330 Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
(2 Lecture, 3 Laboratory – 3 Credits)

Prerequisite: COSC 202, MATH 204 or Math 211, COSC 230 

Course Catalog Description:
This course allows the students to explore the foundational principles and contemporary advancements in Artificial Intelligence (AI). It presents the historical evolution of AI alongside practical problem-solving methodologies like search algorithms and knowledge representation. The course covers intelligent agents, decision trees, classifiers, in addition to some advanced topics such as game theory, to gain a holistic understanding of AI's diverse applications and methodologies. 

Textbook:
Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach by Peter Norvig and Stuart Russell. 4th Edition, 2021,ISBN-10 – 1292401133 ; ISBN-13 - 978-1292401133

Reference Materials:
Artificial Intelligence: A System Approach by M. Tim Jones. Publisher: Jones and Barlett Publishers, 1st Edition, 2008, ISBN: 9780763773373.

Artificial Intelligence, Structures and Strategies for Complex Problem Solving by George F. Luger. Publisher: Addison – Wesley, 6th Edition, 2008 , ISBN-13: 978-0321545893

Course Topics:
AI: General introduction and overview (2 lectures)
Intelligent agents and distinguished environments. ( 2 lectures)
Uninformed Search Techniques (3 lectures)
Informed search techniques (4 lectures)
Knowledge/rule-based representation and reasoning (2 lectures)
Decision tree (4 lectures)
Classification: linear classifier (3 lectures)
Naïve Bayes Classifier (3 lectures)
Artificial Neural Networks: Multi-Layer Perceptron, Backpropagation algorithm. (4 lectures) 
Game theory (3 lectures)

Course Structure:
Lectures are 2 × 50 minutes per week. The laboratory session is 1 × 150 minutes per week. Every newly taught topic is followed by a tutorial and a problem-solving session to encourage student participation. Coursework comprises quizzes and mini-project homework to provide students with a self-learning environment. Laboratory sessions follow the lectures to provide practical examples of theoretical topics.
