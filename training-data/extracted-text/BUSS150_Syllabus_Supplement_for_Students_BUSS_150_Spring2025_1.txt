Instructor Policy on late submission of assignments:
No make-up class tests (i.e., Test 1-4 as stated above) will be given. If a student misses a class test, the weight of the missed class test will be transferred to the Final Exam. 
As per University policy, a documented justifiable missed Mid-Semester Exam will receive the same grade as attained on the Final Exam (if the absence on the Mid-Semester Exam is approved by the University). An unapproved absence for the Mid-Semester Exam is not considered a justification and will be graded zero.
A make-up Final Exam will only be given if approved by the Registrar's Office for documented justifiable reasons. An unapproved absence for the Final exam is not considered a justification and will be graded zero (i.e. no make-up Final exam will be given).

Additional Information:
In-Class Tests:
There will be four in-class tests during the semester. All of the tests will be 40 minutes in length and will cover material you should have mastered since the previous test, but do require you to understand and make use of material taught since the beginning of the course.
Final Exam:
The final exam will cover material from the entire course. The date for the final examination is determined by the Registrar's Office. 
Calculator Policy:
Non-programmable calculators may be used during the in-class tests and exams. Use of any other electronic device, including programmable calculators, during exams will result in an automatic zero score on the exam. Students are not allowed to share calculators.
