Logistics – Examinations
1. Quizzes has been announced in advance. Missing any examination (e.g., quiz, midterm,
etc.) will result in a grade of 0.
2. In case a student has a justification for the absence (e.g., medical report), this should be
directed to the registration office (AND NOT THE INSTRUCTOR) per the university
policy guidelines.
3. The registration will then contact the instructor to inform him/her whether the justification
was accepted or not. Only in that case, the portion of the grade for the examination (e.g.,
5% for a quiz) will be moved to the grade of the final exam.
4. There will be no make-ups exams.
