MATH 204 Linear Algebra
(3 Lecture – 3 Credits)
Prerequisite: MATH 112
Course Catalog Description:
This course is an introduction to Linear Algebra, covering matrices, determinants, systems of
linear equations, vector spaces, linear transformations, eigenvalues, and orthogonality. Students
explore solving systems of linear equations, understanding vector spaces, and analyzing linear
transformations. Additionally, they explore the concepts of eigenvalues and eigenvectors, gaining
insight into their significance within linear algebraic structures.
Textbook:
Elementary Linear Algebra by Howard Anton, Publisher: Wiley, 12th Edition, 2019 ISBN:
9781119268048.
Reference Materials:
th

● Differential Equations and Linear Algebra by C. Edwards and D. Penney, Publisher:
Pearson, 4th Edition, 2017. ISBN: 9780134497181.
Course Topics:
1. Matrices, inverses, and Gaussian elimination(6 lectures)
2. Determinants (6 lectures)
3. Vector spaces, linear combinations, and linear dependence (6 lectures)
4. Basis, dimension, subspaces, rank, and nullity (6 lectures)
5. Column space, row space, and null spaces(6 lectures)
6. Linear transformations and matrix representation (5 lectures)
7. Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and diagonalization (5 lectures)
8. Inner products and orthogonality (3 lectures)
9. Gram-Schmidt process (2 lectures)
Structure and Learning Methodologies:
Lectures are either 3 x 50 minutes per week or 2 x 75 minutes per week. Lectures are presented
using the whiteboard and may be complemented with handouts and/or PowerPoint slides.
Assessment:
All course learning outcomes are assessed using the following assessment tools.
Coursework (Quizzes) 40%
Semester Examination 25%
Final Examination 35%

Calculator Policy
You may only use a basic scientific calculator to help you with arithmetic computations in the MATH-
204 course. The calculator must not be able to perform any matrix operations nor have a graphing
capability. A good example of an appropriate calculator is the Casio fx-82ES. Hence, it is not
permitted to use the calculator to perform any operations involving matrices.
You will be permitted to only use a basic calculator in the Quizzes, Midterm Examination and the
Final Examination. Please do not try to use a more powerful calculator in the Quizzes and
Examinations as it will be confiscated.
Mobile phones may not be used as calculators in the Quizzes, Midterm Examination or Final
Examination. Mobile phones must be switched off during quizzes and exams and must be put away
for the duration of the quiz/exam.
