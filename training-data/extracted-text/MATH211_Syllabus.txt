MATH 211 Differential Equations and Linear Algebra 
(4 Lecture, 0 Laboratory – 4 Credits)

Prerequisite: MATH 112
Co-requisite: None

Course Catalog Description: 
This course introduces ordinary differential equations with linear algebra. The course covers basic topics of linear algebra, including linear systems, basic properties of matrices, vector spaces, and eigenvalues and eigenvectors. The course also covers solution techniques for first-order differential equations, higher-order linear equations with constant coefficients, linear and almost linear systems of differential equations, and Laplace transforms.

Textbook:
Differential Equations and Linear Algebra by C. Edwards, D. Penney and David Calvis, Publisher: Pearson, 4th Global Edition, e-book, 2020, ISBN: 9780137313617.

Reference Materials:
Differential Equations and Linear Algebra by Gilbert Strang, Publisher: Wellesley-Cambridge, UK edition, 2015, ISBN: 9780980232790.

Course Topics:
Solving systems of linear algebraic equations (6 lectures)
Basic matrix properties, determinant, and inverse of a matrix (6 lectures)
Vector spaces and subspaces, row space, null space, and column space (6 lectures)
Basis, dimension, rank, and nullity (4 lectures)
Eigenvalues and eigenvectors (2 lectures)
Classification of differential equations (1 lecture)
First-order differential equations (8 lectures)
Second-order ordinary differential equations (8 lectures)
Applications of second-order ordinary differential equations (4 lectures)
Systems of linear differential equations (5 lectures)
Laplace transforms (5 lectures)
Solving initial value problems using Laplace transforms (5 lectures)

Structure and Learning Methodologies:

Calculator Policy

You may use a basic scientific calculator to help you with arithmetic computations in this course. The calculator must not have a graphing capability. The calculator must not be capable of performing symbolic manipulation. The calculator must not be capable of computing derivatives and integrals. A good example of an appropriate calculator is the Casio fx-82ES.

You will be permitted to use your (basic) calculator in the Quizzes, Midterm Examination and the Final Examination. Please do not try to use a more powerful calculator in the Quizzes and Examinations as it will be confiscated.

Mobile phones may not be used as calculators in the Quizzes, Midterm Examination or Final Examination. Mobile phones must be switched off during quizzes and exams and must be put away for the duration of the quiz/exam.
