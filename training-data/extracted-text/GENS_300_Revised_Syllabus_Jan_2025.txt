GENS 300 Career Preparation
(0 Lecture hours, 2 Studio hours, 1 Credit)
Prerequisites
• GENS 100, Sophomore Standing
Course Catalog Description
This course equips learners with the mindset and skills for both internships and future careers. It includes
employability workshops, group discussions, and one-on-one guidance from a local employability and labor market
specialist. This activity-based course is focused on field exploration, CV and digital profile development, and
professional communication. Course delivery is a blend of in-person and asynchronous online learning experiences.
Reference Materials
K. Brooks, What Color Is Your Parachute? for College. New York, NY, USA: Ten Speed Press, 2021, ISBN: 978-1-9848-
5756-9.
Course Structure & Learning Methodology
Studio sessions are 1 x 100 minutes per week. Sessions involve interactive activities in a blended learning
environment that includes online lessons and classroom lectures facilitated by the course instructor. Assessments
will be formative via online knowledge checks, digital portfolios, peer assessment, and also include an online
summative assessment.

Course Expectations & Conduct:
• Attendance Policy for When Lateness Becomes an Absence & Missing Class Assessments
Students should be in the classroom when the class starts. If students are more than five minutes late, they will be
marked absent. Students are also expected to stay for the full class period. If students leave early or are out of the
class for extended periods of time, they will also be marked absent.
Any class assessments that are missed because of absences will receive a zero unless there is an approved excused
absence form provided by Student Success.
• Late Submission of Assignments
Late assignments will not be accepted and will receive a zero. All assignments must be submitted on Blackboard.
