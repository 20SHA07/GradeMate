Course Catalog Description:
The course acquaints students with the geographical, cultural, demographical, economic and
social aspects of UAE society. It highlights the evolution of the country from a traditional to a
modern society. Topics such as UAE political system, family, population, migration, youth, and
the development of civil society are addressed.

Pedagogy – Learning Process:
• The course will be delivered through the following methodologies: lectures, class
discussions, presentations, films, documentaries, photos, illustrations and maps, internet
resources, field trips.
• Presentations, when used, will only display illustrations and titles, as there should be no
summaries for the content.
• Due to the nature of this course, it is important to depend more on discussions within the
classroom. The instructor is therefore responsible for encouraging students’ participation.
