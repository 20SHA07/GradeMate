Instructor Policy on Late Submission of Assignments:
Quizzes: There will be 4 descriptive quizzes. There will be no makeup quiz. If you miss a quiz, it is your responsibility to contact the registration office and provide them with a legitimate excuse (medical note, for example), and take their approval. 
WAs: WA for each chapter will be provided on Cengage online to be attempted. Please complete the task before the deadline. No extension on the deadline. 
Midterm (MT): MT Exam will be descriptive, and there will be no make-up MT. If you miss this test and you have a valid excuse, the final exam will carry all the point weight of the combined midterm and the final exam (e.g. 50%).
Final Exam: Final Exam will be will be descriptive, and will be scheduled by the registration office.
