COSC 202 Data Science and Artificial Intelligence
(2 Lecture 3 Laboratory– 3 Credits)
Prerequisite: COSC 114
Course Catalog Description:
The course provides a comprehensive introduction to data science and Artificial Intelligence
(AI), emphasizing practical applications and hands-on implementation. Students are introduced
to fundamental concepts spanning the data science and AI pipeline, from data preprocessing to
machine learning techniques. Students gain hands-on experience with real-world datasets and
learn to develop, evaluate, and select machine learning models. The course includes critical
discussions on ethical and privacy considerations and information security measures, including
data security, privacy, opacity, bias, and machine ethics.

Course Topics:
1. Introduction to Computer Science, Data Science and AI: History of Data Science and
AI, Data Science and AI Applications and Trends (2 lectures)
2. Introduction to fundamentals of data science: essential data structures, data pre-
processing and cleaning, exploratory data analysis, examples of features, and data
visualization (6 lectures)
3. Introduction to machine learning: overview of ML pipeline, challenges of ML,
introduction to regression (with linear regression) (5 lectures)
4. Introduction to classification (3 lectures)
5. Introduction to neural networks: multi-layer perceptron, activation functions,
backpropagation algorithm (6 lectures)
6. Introduction to unsupervised learning and k-means clustering (2 lectures)
7. Introduction to deep learning: convolutional neural networks and overview of
generative AI applications and implications (3 lectures)
8. Ethics and privacy in AI, and information security: privacy and surveillance,
manipulation of behavior, opacity, bias, autonomous systems, machine ethics, and
information security (3 lectures)
Course Structure and Methodology:
Lectures are 2 x 50 minutes; Laboratory sessions are 1 x 150 min per week. The course is
delivered through classroom lectures, problem-solving sessions, and laboratory work.
Individual assignments and quizzes are held throughout the semester to reinforce knowledge
of the material.
