TEACHING PLAN (Lecture):
Teaching Plan
Week 1 Introduction: Automata, computability and complexity; mathematical background;
mathematical proofs (Sec. 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4)
Week 2 Finite automata: examples; definitions; designing finite automata (Sec. 1.1)
Week 3 Nondeterministic finite automata; regular expressions (Sec 1.2, 1.3)
Week 4 Pumping lemma; context free grammars (Sec 1.4, 2.1)
Week 5 Pushdown automata; Turing machines (Sec 2.2, 3.1)
Week 6 Variants of Turing machines and algorithms (Sec. 3.2, 3.3)
Week 7 Decidable languages (Sec 4.1)
Week 8 Decidable languages (Sec 4.1)
Week 9 Reducibility: Undecidable Problems from Language Theory, Reductions via
computation histories (Sec. 5.1)
Week 10 Post correspondence problem (Sec. 5.2)
Week 11 Mapping reducibility; computable functions (Sec. 5.3)
Week 12 The recursion theorem (Sec. 6.1)
Week 13 Measuring complexity; the class P (Sec. 7.1, 7.2)
Week 14 The class NP; NP-completeness (Sec 7.3, 7.4)
Week 15 Additional NP-complete problems (Sec. 7.4, 7.5)
This is a tentative schedule and may be adjusted as required.
NOTE: The Teaching Plan is subject to change, please check regularly for updates in RED font.
