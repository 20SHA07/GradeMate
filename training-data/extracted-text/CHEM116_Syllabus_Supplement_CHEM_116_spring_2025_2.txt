Instructor Policy on late submission of assignments:
If the student is absent for a Midterm Exam or the Final Exam, he/she must apply KU rules and regulations to obtain an approval of the excuse. 
If a valid excuse is provided for a missed Midterm Exam, either the percentage for that exam will be added to the percentage for the Final Exam or a make-up exam will be scheduled.
Quiz attendance must be in your own lecture section. You may not attend a quiz in another section. 
Unless a valid excuse is provided, a mark of zero will be assigned for missed quizzes. 
For each lab report, failure to submit it by deadline results in zero mark. There is no make-up for pre-lab quizzes. Cheating in a lab report results in zero.
