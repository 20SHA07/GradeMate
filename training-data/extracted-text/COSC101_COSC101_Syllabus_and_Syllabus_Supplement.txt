COSC 101 Foundations of Computer Science 
(2 Lecture, 3 Laboratory – 3 Credits) 

Prerequisite: COSC 114

Course Catalog Description: 
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to computer science, designed to give students a broad understanding of the field. The aim is to explore various topics, including number systems, data storage, data operations, and computer organization. It also equips students with a foundation and practical knowledge of operating systems, algorithms, data structures, and the social and ethical issues in computing. In addition, the C language is introduced to the students.

Textbooks: 
Foundations of Computer Science by Behrouz Forouzan, Publisher: Cengage, 5th Edition, 2023. ISBN-10: 1473787327, ISBN-13: 978-1473787322.
C Programming Absolute Beginner's Guide by Dean Miller and Greg Perry, Que Corporation, 3rd Edition, 2013. ISBN-10: ‎0789751984, ISBN-13: ‎978-0789751980.

Course Topics: 
Introduction (2 lectures) (Forouzan, Chapter 1)
C programming basics, input/output (2 lectures) (Miller and Perry, Part I, II)
Number systems (2 lectures) (Forouzan, Chapter 2)
Data types and operators, control structures (condition) (2 lectures) (Miller and Perry, Part II, III)
Data storage (2 lectures) (Forouzan, Chapter 3)
Operations on data (2 lectures) (Forouzan, Chapter 4)
Control structures (repetition/loops) (2 lectures) (Miller and Perry, Part III)
Computer organization (2 lectures) (Forouzan, Chapter 5)
Data structures (2 lectures) (Forouzan, Chapter 12)
Arrays and pointers (2 lectures) (Miller and Perry, Part IV)
Dynamic memory allocation and functions (2 lectures) (Miller and Perry, Part IV, V) 
Recursion; file handling (2 lectures) (Miller and Perry, Part V)
Operating systems (2 lectures) (Forouzan, Chapter 7)
Algorithms (2 lectures) (Forouzan, Chapter 8)
Social and ethical issues (2 lectures) (Forouzan, Chapter 22)

Structure and learning methodologies: 
Lectures are 2 x 50 minutes; Laboratory sessions are 1 x 150 min per week. The course is delivered through classroom lectures, problem-solving sessions, and laboratory work done individually. Individual assignments and quizzes are held throughout the semester to reinforce knowledge of the material.
