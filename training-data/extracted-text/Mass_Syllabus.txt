Instructor Policy on Assessments:
• The solutions to assigned problems will not be collected and graded. Students are highly
encouraged to spend the time to work out the problems. The primary purpose of the assigned
problems is to develop problem solving skills, think more deeply about the course material, and
prepare for upcoming quizzes and exams. Effort given for studying and doing the problems in
this course is directly related to performance in the quizzes and exams, and as a result in the final
grade.
• If a student believes that there might be an error in grading a quiz or an exam, he/she should see
the instructor within one (1) week after the graded assessment is returned. It should be noted that
the entire exam will be re-graded not just the problem in question, in order to ensure consistence
and fairness. Final course grades may only be contested by following the approved academic
appeal procedures.
• There will be no make-up quizzes. A make-up exam will be allowed under extenuating
circumstance, such as hospitalization or bereavement. Even in these situations, students should
realize that the instructor has the right not to accept an authenticated excuse. The excuse must be
authenticated by Student Success Department and brought to the instructor immediately (within

TEACHING PLAN (Lecture):
Weeks 1-2 Fundamentals of diffusion and diffusion coefficients
Weeks 3-5 Mass transfer coefficients and convective mass transfer
Weeks 6-8 Interphase mass transfer
Weeks 9-11 Mass balances
Weeks 12-14 Absorption and stripping
Weeks 15-16 Combined mass transfer and reaction kinetics
