COSC 202 Data Science and Artificial Intelligence 
(2 Lecture 3 Laboratory– 3 Credits) 
 
Prerequisite: COSC 114 
 
Course Catalog Description: 
The course is designed to give engineers a comprehensive understanding of the key concepts, techniques, tools, and applications of data science and Artificial Intelligence (AI). The course focuses on applications in various engineering domains. Course topics include data analysis and visualization, machine learning, deep learning, applications, natural language processing, computer vision, autonomous driving, and ethics considerations. Students will learn how these concepts can be applied to real-world problems and gain hands-on experience with popular AI and data science tools and libraries. 

Textbook: 

Artificial Intelligence with Python, by Alberto Artasanchez and Prateek Joshi. Publisher: Packt Publishing Ltd, 2nd Edition, 2020. ISBN-10: 183921953X. ISBN-13: 978-1839219535.

Reference Materials: 
Introduction to Machine Learning with Python: A Guide for Data Scientists by Andreas C. Müller and Sarah Guido. Publisher: O'Reilly Media, 1st edition, 2016, ISBN-10: 1449369413, ISBN-13: 978-1449369415.
First Principles with Python by Joel Grus, Data Science from Scratch. Publisher: O’Reilly Media; 2nd Edition, 2019. ISBN-10: 1492041130. ISBN-13: 978-1492041139.

Course Topics: 
Introduction to Computer Science, Data Science and AI: History of Data Science and AI, Data Science and AI Applications and Trends (2 lectures)
Data types and structures: Numerical data, categorical data, text data, data structures such as arrays, tables, and graphs (2 lectures) 
Introduction to fundamentals of data science: Problem-solving with data, data collection, data pre-processing, exploratory data analysis, data acquisition, data cleaning, examples of features, and data visualization (6 lectures) 
Introduction to machine learning: supervised learning with applications, unsupervised learning with applications (4 lectures) 
Introduction to deep learning: introduction to neural networks, application of Conventional Neural Networks (CNN), applications of Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN) (4 lectures)
Introduction to Natural Language Processing: Application 1: Text processing, Application 2: Sentiment analysis (4 lectures)
Applications of AI and Data Science: Computer vision, Speech recognition, Autonomous driving (6 lectures)
Ethics and Privacy in AI and Data Science: Bias in Data and Algorithms, Privacy and Security (2 lectures)

Course Structure and Methodology: 
Lectures are 2 x 50 minutes; Laboratory sessions are 1 x 150 min per week. The course is delivered through classroom lectures, problem-solving sessions, and laboratory work. Individual assignments and quizzes are held throughout the semester to reinforce knowledge of the material.
