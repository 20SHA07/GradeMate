Additional Information:
Assessment
There are 2 quizzes scheduled in this course. Each quiz will therefore count for 10% of your
final grade for the course. There will be no make-up quizzes. If you fail to attend a scheduled quiz
for any reason, then you will receive a grade of zero for that quiz.
Assignment
In the week 3 of the semester, students will be provided an assignment in groups and they will
have to submit the week before the Midterm. Each group will be composed of 3 or 4 students and
will have a different data set. The assignment must be submitted in the week 8 of the semester. If
the report is submitted late, the project grade will be decreased by 10% for each day of delay.
Project
In the week 11 of the semester, students will be assigned a project regarding data analysis using a
statistical software. Each group will be composed of 3 or 4 students and will have a different data
set. A final report on the data analysis must be submitted in the week 15 of the semester. If the
report is submitted late, the project grade will be decreased by 10% for each day of delay.
Calculator Policy
You may use a basic scientific calculator to help you with arithmetic calculations. Calculators with
any sort of graphing, symbolic manipulation or integral and derivative calculation capabilities
are strictly prohibited. Any such device found with a student during any form of assessment will
be treated as a violation of the above-stated Honor Code Academic Pledge.
You will be permitted to only use a basic calculator in the Quizzes, Semester Examination and the
Final Examination.
Mobile phones may not be used as calculators in the Quizzes, Semester Examination or Final
Examination. Mobile phones must be switched off during quizzes and exams and must be put
away for the duration of the quiz/exam.
Classroom Policies
Discipline is one of the main components of your academic and professional success.
Please arrive at the class periods in a timely fashion. Classes will start as scheduled. Students
arriving late to class are a distraction to both the instructor and the other students in the classroom.
As per the University policies, any student arriving late to class is to be considered absent.
Please ensure that you sign your name on the Attendance Sheet which is handed-out at the
beginning of each class period. If you do not sign the Attendance Sheet, then you will be marked
“Absent” in the Banner.
If you are absent for any lecture, it is your responsibility to cover the missed material.
Homework Policy
You are expected to solve all examples presented in the classroom as well as exercises at the end
of each section of the book. Homework solutions will not be collected-in nor graded. Homework
is your responsibility. Assistance is readily available from the Instructor at his Office Hours.
