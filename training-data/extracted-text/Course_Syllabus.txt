BMED 352 Fundamentals of Biomedical Signal Processing
(3 Lectures, 3 Laboratories – 4 Credits)
Prerequisite: BMED 351
Course Catalog Description:
The primary objective of this course is to study analogue and digital signal processing techniques
and microcomputer system, and their application to biomedical instrumentation and physiological
measurements. This course is designed for students who are expected to have prior knowledge in
circuits and physiological system modelling. The main focus is on the technical aspects of
biosignal processing and its hardware implementation in medical instruments.

Course topics:
1. Introduction to continuous signals (4 lectures)
2. Fourier transform of continuous signal (3 lectures)
3. Practical issues of sampling and discretization of continuous signal (2 lectures)
4. Introduction to discrete signals (2 lectures)
5. Discrete Fourier Transform and frequency responses (4 lectures)
6. Z-transform and transfer function representation of discrete time system (6 lectures)
7. Stability and transient response of transfer function (2 lectures)
8. Closed loop control system (3 lecture)
9. Discrete system convolution (finite and infinite response systems) (2 lectures)
10. Design of Digital filters (4 lectures)
11. Noise removal and signal enhancement (5 lectures)
12. Examples of AI based models in biomedical devices (2 lectures)
13. Functional organization of a microcontroller (2 lectures)
14. Functional organization of a medical device (2 lectures)
15. Practical Issues in implementing digital signal processing systems in medical devices (2
lectures)
Laboratory experiments:
1. Introduction to MATLAB signal processing toolbox and Simulink
2. Design of a simple discrete system in MATLAB Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) to analyze
frequency spectrum of bio signals
