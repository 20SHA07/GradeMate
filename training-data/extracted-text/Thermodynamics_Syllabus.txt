CHEG 230 Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics I
(3 Lectures – 3 Credits)
Prerequisite: CHEG 205
Co-requisite: PHYS 122
Course Catalog Description:
Fundamentals of classical thermodynamics for application to chemical engineering processes and
systems. Application of first and second laws to the analysis of thermodynamic cycles and processes;
volumetric and thermodynamic properties of liquids and gases; heat effects.
Textbook:
Introduction to Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics, J. M. Smith, H. C. Van Ness, and M. M.
Abbott, 8th edition, McGraw-Hill, New York, 2018, ISBN: 978-1-259-92189-6.
Reference Materials:
No Reference Materials
Course Topics
1. Introduction to thermodynamics (5 lectures)
2. First law of thermodynamics (6 lectures)
3. PVT behavior of pure fluids (9 lectures)
4. Heat effects (6 lectures)
5. Second law of thermodynamics (6 lectures)
6. Applications of first and second law to flow processes (4 lectures)
7. Thermodynamic properties of fluids (9 lectures)
Course structure and Learning Methodologies:
The course involves lectures (either 2x75 or 3x50 minutes per week) supported with PowerPoint
presentations. In several instances, the students will solve in-class exercises during the lecture hours.
Moreover, students will be given homework assignments.
Assessment:
All course learning outcomes are assessed using the following assessment tools.
Coursework (Quizzes, homework, projects) 30%
Semester Examination 30%
Final Examination 40%
