BUSS 322 Fundamentals of Innovation & Entrepreneurship
(3 Lectures, 0 Laboratory – 3 Credits)
Prerequisite: Junior Standing
Co-requisite: None
Course Catalogue Description:
This course introduces students to an innovative and entrepreneurial mindset, which will help them develop a creative
problem-solving approach to challenges. Students design an opportunity analysis and a personal business plan, allowing them
to find better solutions to challenges in their career and life.
Textbook:
Technology Ventures: From Idea to Enterprise by Tom Byers, Dorf, and Nelson, Publisher: McGraw Hill Education, 4th Edition,
2015, International Student Edition, ISBN: 9781259875991
Reference Materials:
1. Change by Design: How Design Thinking Transforms Organizations and Inspires Innovation by Tim Brown, Publisher:
HarperCollins, 1st Edition, 2009, ISBN: 9780061766084
2. Creative Confidence: Unleashing the Creative Potential Within Us All by Tom Kelley and David Kelley, Publisher: Crown
Business, 2013, ISBN: 9780385349369
Course Topics:
PHASE I: INTRODUCTION TO INNOVATION
Session 1A: The innovative mindset (1.5 lectures)
Session 1B: Where ideas come from (1.5 lectures)
Session 2A: Creative confidence (1.5 lectures)
Session 2B: The Design Thinking process (1.5 lectures)
Session 3A: Need finding and empathy (1.5 lectures)
Session 3B: Generating insights and reframing (1.5 lectures)
Session 4A: Idea generation (1.5 lectures)
Session 4B: Prototyping and experimentation (1.5 lectures)
Session 5A: Influencing and inspiring others (1.5 lectures)
Session 5B: Leading for creative confidence and going global (1.5 lectures)
Sessions 6A/B: Design Thinking in practice (3 lectures)
PHASE II: INTRODUCTION TO ENTREPRENEURSHIP
Session 7A: The entrepreneurial mindset (vision, mission, and strategy) (1.5 lectures)
Session 7B: How an entrepreneur identifies ideas and opportunities (1.5 lectures)
Session 8A: Lean startup methods I (1.5 lectures)
Session 8B: Lean startup methods II (1.5 lectures)
Session 9A: Go-to-market methods (1.5 lectures)
Session 9B: Opportunity analysis in practice (1.5 lectures)
Session 10A: Sources of capital for entrepreneurs (1.5 lectures)
Session 10B: How entrepreneurs assemble and motivate a Team (1.5 lectures)
Session 11A: Legal matters in startups (1.5 lectures)
Session 11B: Pitching and presenting as an entrepreneur (1.5 lectures)
Sessions 12A/B: Opportunity analysis project (3 lectures)

Structure and Learning Methodologies:
Lectures will be 2 x 75 minutes per week.
Interactive and participatory learner-centered instructional methods will be utilized to explore content focused themes.
Each session will include a mix of the following components: mini-lectures, discussion, interactive activities, and open Q&A.
