TEACHING PLAN (Tentative Lectures):
Week 1 (25 Aug – 1 Sep) Introduction: Review of Object-Oriented Design.
*25 Aug - Classes Begin
*1 Sep - End of Add/Drop
Week 2 (1 – 5 Sep) Algorithm Complexity Performance of Algorithms, Time and Space tradeoff.
Worst-case and Average-case performance.
Week 3 (8 – 12 Sep) The big- Oh Notation, Complexity Calculations examples.
Week 4 (15 – 19 Sep) NP completeness and approximation algorithms.
Week 5 (22 – 26 Sep) Fundamental Data Structures: Concept of Abstract Data Types (ADTs)
(Quiz 1 – Thu 25 Sep).
Week 6 (29 Sep – 3 Oct) ADT specification: Unsorted and Sorted Lists.
Week 7 (6 – 10 Oct) Stacks.
Week 8 (13 – 17 Oct) Queues (Quiz 2 – Thu 16 Oct).
Week 9 (20 – 24 Oct) Sorting and Searching Algorithms: Sorting: Behavior of Sorting Algorithms,
Selection Sort, Insertion Sort, Bubble Sort. Midterm Exam 21/23 Oct
Week 10 (27 – 31 Oct) Quick Sort, Hashing, Radix Sort. Assignment Issue – Wed 29 Oct
*31 Oct- Last day to drop w/”W”
Week 11 (3 – 7 Nov) Heap Sort, Merge Sort.
Week 12 (10 – 14 Nov) Binary Search Trees.
Week 13 (17 – 21 Nov) Advanced Data Structures: Priority Queues, Heaps (Quiz 3 – Thu 20 Nov).
Week 14 (24 – 28 Nov) Graphs & Sets Algorithms Assignment Deadline: Thu 27 Nov at 11:59 PM
Week 15 (1 Dec – 5 Dec) Graphs & Sets Algorithms.
*5 Dec - Last Day of Classes
*18 Dec - Final Grades Due
