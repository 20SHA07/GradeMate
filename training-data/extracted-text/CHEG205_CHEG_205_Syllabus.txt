TEACHING PLAN (Lecture):
Teaching Topic(s)
Week
1 Introduction to chemical engineering; dimensions &units, unit conversion; dimensional
homogeneity & dimensionless numbers; data analysis, representation, fitting
2 Process variables: Mass, volume, specific gravity, flow rate, chemical composition, mole
and mass fractions, pressure, temperature
3 Material Balances: basic process units; flowcharts, continuous, batch, semi-batch
processes; basis of calculation; degree of freedom analysis
4 Multi-unit processes. Recycle, bypass
5 Reactive processes: stoichiometry; conversion, extent of reaction; chemical equilibrium;
multiple reactions
6 Atomic species balances; Combustion
7 Single-phase systems: PVT relations; ideal gas; gas mixtures; real gas equations of state;
corresponding states; compressibility factor
8 Multi-phase systems: Phase equilibria; phase diagrams; vapor pressure; the Gibbs phase
rule; single-condensable component VLE; humidity
9 Multi-component VLE; Raoult’s & Henry’s Laws; bubble point & dew point; VLE
phase diagrams; solutions; solubility; liquid miscibility; ternary phase diagrams;
adsorption
10 Energy and energy balances: Forms of energy; 1st law of thermodynamics; energy
balance on closed systems
11 Energy balances on open systems; Enthalpy; steam tables; mechanical energy balance;
Bernoulli equation
12 Nonreactive processes; hypothetical paths for ΔH determination; pressure & temperature
changes; heat capacity & sensible heats
13 Phase changes; latent heats; humidification; psychometric charts; heat of mixing and
solution; enthalpy-concentration charts; combined materials & energy balances
14 Energy balances on reactive processes: Heat of reaction; Hess’s law; heat of formation;
ΔH by heat of reaction & heat of formation approaches
15 Fuels & combustion; adiabatic flame temperature
