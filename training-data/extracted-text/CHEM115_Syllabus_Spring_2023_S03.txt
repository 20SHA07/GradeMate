Instructor Policy on late Submission of Assignments:
 If the student is absent for a Midterm Exam, the Final Exam or a Laboratory Session, he/she must
apply KU rules and regulations to obtain an approval of the excuse.
 If a valid excuse is provided for a missed Midterm Exam, either the percentage for that exam will
be added to the percentage for the Final Exam or a make-up exam will be scheduled.
 Quiz attendance must be in your own lecture/lab section.
 A mark of zero will be assigned for each missed quiz in either lecture or lab, if a valid excuse is not
provided. Please note that no make-up quizzes will be given.
 Lowest quiz will drop off
For each pre-lab quiz and lab report, failure to submit it by the deadline results in a zero mark. There is
no make-up for pre-lab quizzes. Cheating in any assignment or lab report results in a zero mark.

KU Grading System – Policy ACA 3350
The official Khalifa University of Science and Technology grading system uses letter grades with
pluses and minuses. Passing grades range from A to D; F is a fail grade. E ach letter grade is
assigned a grade point on a fo ur - point scale as set out below:

Very Good

Satisfactory
C 2.00

D 1.00 Poor
F 0.00 Fail
WF 0.00 Withdrew Failing
Additional letter grades are used by the Registrar to denote special cases. These letter grades do
not have corresponding grade points and are not used in calculating a student’s grade point
average.
