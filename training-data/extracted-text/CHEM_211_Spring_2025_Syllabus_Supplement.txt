Instructor Policy on late submission of assignments:
If the student is absent for a Midterm Exam or the Final Exam, he/she must apply KU rules and regulations to obtain an approval of the excuse.
If a valid excuse is provided for a missed Midterm Exam, the percentage for that exam will be added to the percentage for the Final Exam.
Quiz attendance must be in your own lecture section. You may not attend a quiz in another section.
A mark of zero will be assigned for each missed quiz, whether a valid excuse is presented or not. Please note that no make-up quizzes will be given –quiz grade will be based on the best 4 marks.
For each pre-lab quiz and lab report, failure to submit it by the deadline results in a zero mark. There is no make-up for pre-lab quizzes.
For each lab quiz and lab report, failure to submit it by the deadline will result in a mark of zero. There is no make-up for lab quizzes.
