MATH 232 Engineering Mathematics
(3 Lectures, 0 Laboratories – 3 Credits)
Prerequisite: MATH 112
Co-requisite: None
Course Catalog Description:
This course covers selected topics from mathematical analysis with engineering applications, including
complex numbers, partial derivatives, gradient vectors, multiple integrals, and Fourier series.

Course Topics:
1. Introduction to Complex Numbers [Stewart Appendix ]. (6 lectures)
a. Complex arithmetic, complex argument, and polar form.
b. Complex powers, roots, trigonometric and exponential functions.
2. Partial Derivatives [Stewart 14.1-14.7]. (12 lectures)
a. Functions of several variables.
b. Limits and continuity (examples only).
c. Partial derivatives.
d. Tangent Planes and Linear Approximation (examples only).
e. Chain Rule.
f. Gradient vector and directional derivatives.
g. Minimum and Maximum values.
3. Multiple Integrals [Stewart 15.1-15.9]. (12 lectures)
a. Double integrals over rectangles.
b. Iterated integrals.
c. Double integrals over general regions.
d. Double integrals in polar coordinates.
e. Applications of double integrals.
f. Surface area.
g. Triple integrals in cylindrical and spherical coordinates.
4. Fourier Analysis [Kreyszig 11.1-11.4, 11.7-11.9]. (15 lectures)
a. Introduction to Fourier Series.
b. Periodic functions, Fourier sine and cosine series.
c. Complex Fourier series, Parseval’s theorem.
d. Fourier integrals, Fourier cosine and sine transforms.
e. Complex Fourier transform, convolution.
Note: One lecture is a 50 minute class period.
