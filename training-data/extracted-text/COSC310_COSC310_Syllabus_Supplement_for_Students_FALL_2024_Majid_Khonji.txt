TEACHING PLAN (Lecture):
Week 1 (26 Aug - 30 Aug) Introduction: Review of Object-Oriented Design.
*26 Aug - Classes Begin
*30 Aug - End of Add/Drop
Week 2 (2 – 6 Sept) Algorithm Complexity Performance of Algorithms, Time and Space tradeoff.
Worst-case and Average-case performance.
Week 3 (9 – 13 Sept) The big- Oh Notation, Complexity Calculations examples.
Week 4 (16 – 20 Sept) NP completeness and approximation algorithms.
Week 5 (23 – 27 Sept) Fundamental Data Structures: Concept of Abstract Data Types (ADTs)
(Quiz 1 – Thu 26 Sept).
Week 6 (30 Sep – 4 Oct) ADT specification: Unsorted and Sorted Lists.
Week 7 (9 – 13 Oct) Stacks.
Week 8 (16 – 20 Oct) Queues (Quiz 2 – Thu 17 Oct).
Week 9 (23 – 27 Oct) Sorting and Searching Algorithms: Sorting: Behavior of Sorting Algorithms,
Selection Sort, Bubble Sort. Midterm Exam: Thu 24 Oct
Week 10 (30 Oct – 3 Nov) Quick Sort, Hashing, Radix Sort. Assignment Issue – Thu 31 Oct
*1st Nov- Last day to drop w/”W”
Week 11 (4 – 8 Nov) Heap Sort, Merge Sort.
Week 12 (11 – 15 Nov) Binary Search Trees.
Week 13 (18 – 22 Nov) Advanced Data Structures: Priority Queues, Heaps
(Quiz 3 – Thu 21 Nov).
Week 14 (25 – 29 Nov) Graphs & Sets Algorithms.
Assignment Deadline: Mon 25 Nov at 11:59 PM
Week 15 (2 – 6 Dec) Revision.
*6 Dec - Last Day of Classes
